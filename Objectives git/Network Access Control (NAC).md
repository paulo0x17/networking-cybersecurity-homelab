
## Whats is a NAC?

A NAC is a cybersecurity solution designed to centralize and automate the enforcement of secrurity policies on devices seeking to connect to a network

It's responsible for:

- Identifying and profiling devices
- Authentication and Access Control
- Posture Assassment and Compliance
- Quarentine and Remediation

On my homelab, running a NAC allows me to simulate enterprise-grade network segmentation.
